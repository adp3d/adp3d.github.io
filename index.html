One app, from design to print

You describe what you want. ADP3D Studio builds the model, checks it, prepares it for your printer and keeps an eye on every step so the part comes out right.

  1. Design

    Start from words, a photo, a sketch or one of the ready-made tools.

  2. Check

    Thin walls, gaps, overhangs and loose pieces are found and fixed before printing.

  3. Prepare

    Supports, colours for the AMS and print settings are chosen for the A1 Combo.

  4. Print

    Send the job and follow it. Problems are flagged while there is still time to fix them.

Tools that do the hard part

Each tool makes a print-ready model in seconds. Change a few numbers, see it update, print.

Idea to 3D

Turn a photo or a written idea into a 3D model you can edit. This tool runs on a graphics card when you have one.

Ready-made parts

Gears, nuts and bolts, jars with screw caps, Gridfinity bins and wall panels with exact sizes.

Name plates and stamps

Text in English, Marathi or Hindi, keychains, stamps, stencils and QR plates.

Cookie cutters

Pick a shape or use your own drawing, and get a cutter that prints cleanly.

Multicolour printing

Colour a design and it is split for the AMS, ready to print in several colours.

Export anywhere

Save as STL or 3MF for printing, or DXF and SVG for laser and CNC work.

It watches every step for quality

Most failed prints start with a problem nobody saw. ADP3D Studio checks the model, the settings and the plate before anything reaches the printer, and tells you in plain words what it found.

  • Closed, solid model with no holes or broken faces
  • Walls and details thick enough for the nozzle
  • Fits the bed, or is split into parts that do
  • Supports only where the part really needs them
  • Colours matched to the filament loaded in the AMS
